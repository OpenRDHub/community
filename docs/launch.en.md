## Arrangements for actual participants

1. Confirm contributor, support, reviewer, output, and next check-in on the original Issue before marking it Ready.
2. An authorized `.github` maintainer must apply the prepared homepage patch. mattheliu still has read-only access; the public profile is unchanged.
3. The repository, Issues, PRs, and Discussions are now public under user instruction. Confirm intake contacts and feedback arrangements. The board remains private and organization Discussions is not configured.
4. Choose one real meeting note or edited WeChat topic, confirm its source, reviewer and audience, then submit a records PR. No WeChat or Feishu source material has been imported.
5. Finish one real task through assignment, delivery, feedback, credit and handover; use the experience to revise the process. Participants agree on timing.

## What contributors want in return

Participants can state their interests, availability, support needs, and desired mentoring, credit, evidence of skills, or collaboration opportunities. Resource providers must confirm offers. Record actual support and results without promising payment, jobs, or resources that have not been agreed.
