# Resources and support

Describe the support you can offer or the help you need clearly enough for others to assess a possible match.

## What you can offer

A review session, guidance, documentation help, equipment, making facilities, space, funding, content, or outreach can all be useful. Explain the scope and availability.

Record the support, conditions, amount or available time, expiry date, and how follow-up can be arranged. Keep private contact details out of public records.

## Current availability

No provider-confirmed resources have been entered in this draft. It does not promise mentoring slots, equipment allowances, reimbursement, or job opportunities.

The directory will distinguish Seeking support, Confirmed availability, and Allocated or delivered, with the last confirmation date and actual use.

## Requesting support

Explain what you are working on, what is missing, how you would use the support, and what feedback you can provide. State allocation criteria before distributing scarce resources and record the decision.

## Recognizing support

Agree on acknowledgement, feedback on use, and outcome sharing. Individuals decide whether their identity and credit are public. Sponsorship does not automatically grant authority over all community decisions.
