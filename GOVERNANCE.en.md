# How the community makes decisions

These are proposed working agreements. Actual responsibilities and availability are recorded in [Maintainers](MAINTAINERS.en.md).

## Responsibilities and authority

| Responsibility | Work involved | Decision scope |
|---|---|---|
| Organization administration | Settings, access, and administrator handovers | Organization permissions, repository visibility, and administration |
| Community coordination | Intake, priorities, and work across roles | Work arrangements within the agreed scope |
| Documentation and content | Shared guides, navigation, materials, and updates | Editing and reviewing the assigned materials |
| Contributor support and review | Task preparation, questions, feedback, and handovers | Acceptance of work within an agreed responsibility |
| Needs and resource liaison | Clarifying needs, checking conditions, and following up support | Authorized coordination; no promises on behalf of others |

One person may cover several responsibilities. Unassigned work remains visible; it is not automatically assigned to the coordinator.

## Reaching a decision

The relevant maintainer reviews routine edits. Changes to participation rules, ongoing responsibilities, or shared resources start with a single proposal explaining the problem, options, impact, discussion deadline, and decision owner.

Notify affected people and record differing views and the reason for the decision. Update stable documents afterwards. Silence does not mean agreeing to take on work.

## Disagreements, support, and handovers

Establish the facts and options, then involve the responsible person. A decision involving that person's own interests should be reviewed by another actual responsible person without a direct conflict. Funding, branding, and external commitments require the relevant authorized person.

Recognition does not automatically grant administrative access. Agree on scope and support before handing over the permissions needed for maintenance. Previous contributions remain credited after someone steps down.

## Discussion expectations

Discuss specific problems and respect different professional and lived experiences. Personal information, credit, and case studies are shared according to the person's wishes. A contact and private channel for sensitive feedback must be established before public intake opens; this draft does not invent a contact.
