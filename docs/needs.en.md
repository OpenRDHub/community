# Share a need

Start with a real situation: who encounters the difficulty, what happens, how it is handled today, and what could be improved.

## Useful details

- The situation and current difficulty.
- Existing workarounds and what remains inconvenient.
- One improvement you would prioritize.
- Relevant projects or materials, if any. A suitable feedback route can be agreed later.

You do not need a technical proposal. Public records contain only an appropriate summary, not raw medical records, identity details, or private contact information.

## What happens next

A contact helps clarify the situation and look for similar needs. Link a need to its project when one exists; otherwise retain a community record.

This phase focuses on setting up intake. Recording a need does not approve a project or promise a delivery date. It may be marked Needs clarification, Awaiting a team, or Linked to a project.

## Staying in touch

Feedback methods and timing are voluntary. You may pause or ask a trusted liaison to help. Contacts still need confirmation; see [Maintainers](../MAINTAINERS.en.md).
