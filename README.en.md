# Let's build the community together

This repository holds the participation guides, working agreements, needs intake, and resource directory shared by KHub / OpenRD.

Our current focus is community building. You can help organize materials, improve instructions, try a participation workflow, or welcome others, whether or not you write code.

## Choose your first step

| What brings you here? | Start with |
|---|---|
| I want to help, but I am not sure where to start | [Participation guide](docs/getting-started.en.md) |
| I have found something we could improve | [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) |
| I want to share a need | [Needs intake](docs/needs.en.md) |
| I can offer guidance, equipment, funding, or other support | [Resource collaboration](docs/resources.en.md) |
| I want to understand responsibilities and decisions | [Maintainers](MAINTAINERS.en.md) and [governance](GOVERNANCE.en.md) |

## Our first shared tasks

We plan to improve homepage navigation, newcomer instructions, historical materials, FAQs, needs templates, and resource information. These are candidate tasks; contacts and reviewers must be confirmed before assignment opens.

[View the roadmap](ROADMAP.en.md)

## Where information belongs

- **Documents**: stable guides, directories, and working agreements.
- **Issues**: actionable community work, needs, and resource requests or offers.
- **Discussions**: questions, experiences, and open ideas. [Discussion categories](docs/discussions.en.md).
- **Pull requests**: changes to documents, directories, and templates that need review.
- **Project repositories**: implementation and delivery. Find them in the [project directory](docs/projects.en.md).

Keep one primary record for each actionable task. Link to it from other entry points.

## After a contribution

Give specific feedback and record the result and credit according to the contributor's wishes. People who want to continue can take another task or gradually take responsibility for documentation, onboarding, or review.

[Contribution and growth](MEMBERSHIP.en.md) · [Everyday collaboration](docs/working-together.en.md)
