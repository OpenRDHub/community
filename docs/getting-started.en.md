# Find your first step

You do not need organization membership or coding skills to start. Begin with something small that fits your experience.

## I want to help build the community

Browse the [community roadmap](../ROADMAP.en.md). Documentation, navigation, content checks, indexing, and template testing benefit from different backgrounds.

Contacts for candidate tasks are still being arranged. Describe the part that interests you, your availability, and any help you need. Agree on the next step once a contact and reviewer are confirmed.

## I want to share a need

Read [Needs intake](needs.en.md). Describe the situation, what is difficult, what you do today, and what you hope to improve. A complete technical solution is not required.

## I can offer support

Start with [Resource collaboration](resources.en.md). Describe the specific guidance, materials, equipment, space, funding, content, or outreach help you can offer.

## I am new to GitHub

You can prepare text or materials and ask a confirmed liaison to help record them. Check [Maintainers](../MAINTAINERS.en.md) for current arrangements. The local preview lets you try writing a draft without sending anything to GitHub.
