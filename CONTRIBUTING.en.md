# How to contribute

Start with a clearly scoped task. Small wording fixes can be proposed directly. For a substantial rewrite, a rule change, or a new directory structure, explain the problem first to avoid duplicated effort.

## Before taking a task

1. Agree on the expected outcome and useful reference materials.
2. Confirm who can answer questions, who can review, and when to check progress.
3. Describe your availability and any guidance or resources you need.

You can express interest in an item without a confirmed contact. That does not yet constitute an assignment.

## Submission and feedback

Submit file changes through a pull request linked to the relevant item. Explain what changed, how you checked it, and where you need help. Design, research, and operational suggestions can also be attached to the original item, with help from a liaison when needed.

Review feedback should explain what is accepted, what needs work, and who takes the next step. Record the reason when a suggestion is not adopted.

## Pausing or handing over

Reduce the scope, extend the schedule, or arrange a handover if your availability changes. Leave your current materials, completed work, blockers, and next steps. Pausing does not erase credit for previous contributions.
