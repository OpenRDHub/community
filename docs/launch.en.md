## Arrangements for actual participants

1. Confirm contributor, support, reviewer, output, and next check-in on the original Issue before offering supported assignment. Independent small fixes can go directly to a PR.
2. Confirm whether the existing personal-account needs tracker remains the shared intake route and who triages it. Known project work goes to its project repository.
3. Choose one real meeting note or edited WeChat topic, confirm its source, reviewer, and audience, then submit a records PR. No WeChat or Feishu source material has been imported.
4. Record real resources only after providers confirm the support, conditions, expiry, and contact route. Examples are not available offers.
5. Complete delivery, feedback, credit, and handover for one task. C01 has technical changes ready for independent feedback; participants confirm their own availability.

These steps require real participant input or action. Repository access, board visibility, and profile publishing no longer block them. Public website deployment is separate: the current local build includes working materials and examples and is not a public release package.

## What contributors want in return

Participants can state their interests, availability, support needs, and desired mentoring, credit, evidence of skills, or collaboration opportunities. Resource providers must confirm offers. Record actual support and outcomes without promising unagreed payments, jobs, or resources.
