# Contribution and growth

Your participation can change with your interests and availability. Completing a single task is a valuable contribution.

| Participation | Possible contributions | Recognition |
|---|---|---|
| One contribution | Fix instructions, check navigation, add references, or give useful feedback | Specific feedback and credit according to your wishes |
| Ongoing contributions | Maintain documents, welcome people, check resources, or update content | A record of the work and participation in relevant discussions |
| Maintenance and mentoring | Review work, maintain a shared area, or support newcomers | A clear scope, necessary permissions, and someone to ask for help |
| Needs participation | Explain a situation, correct assumptions, or discuss priorities | Visibility into status, a say in relevant decisions, and the option to pause |
| Mentoring and support | Offer advice, review, equipment, funding, or connections | Agreed acknowledgement and feedback on use and outcomes |

## What to expect

Learning feedback, a record of real work, collaborators, and growing responsibility should be tied to specific tasks. Equipment, reimbursement, awards, or job opportunities enter the [resource directory](docs/resources.en.md) only after confirmation.

Code, documentation, design, testing, needs work, operations, and coordination all count. Points or PR totals do not replace the substance of a contribution. You decide whether your personal information and credit are public.

## Taking on more responsibility

After expressing interest or accepting an invitation, agree on a small maintenance scope and work through a handover with the current maintainer. Update [responsibilities](MAINTAINERS.en.md) and necessary access once both sides agree.

A working group or SIG needs ongoing work, a coordinator, participants, and records. Create one when it is useful; the present focus is shared community collaboration.
