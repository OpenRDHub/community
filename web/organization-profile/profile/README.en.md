## Who We Are

The Khub team was founded in 2024 and launched the OpenRD rare disease open-source community in September 2025. Patients and professionals from different disciplines bring together medicine, technology, design, community operations, education, and communication through open collaboration.

We want people to keep building useful projects together. Patients can define their own needs, help set product priorities, test experiences, and participate as co-creators.

## What We Believe

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| Patients also help create solutions | Patients, families, and clinicians should help define the direction, minimum useful outcome, and acceptance criteria. |
| Rare disease needs extend beyond treatment | Daily life, education, employment, social connection, information, and long-term self-management also matter. |
| Open collaboration makes progress possible | Share problems and capabilities, support handovers, and improve work together. |
| Everyone can contribute | Technical, medical, design, community, communications, education, patient, family, and student perspectives all have a place. |

## How It Works

The existing community model starts with a real need and aims to sustain useful work:

1. Patients or frontline clinicians describe a need.
2. Contributors form a group across development, design, research, fabrication, and communication.
3. The group builds an initial useful version.
4. The people with the need validate the outcome.
5. More patients try it and provide feedback.
6. Resources or proceeds return to support community operations.

Each project has an actual needs partner who helps define its direction and acceptance criteria. Any specific arrangement for income or resource allocation needs an explicit agreement; this diagram is not a promise of returns.
